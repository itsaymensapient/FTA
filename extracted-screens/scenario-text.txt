GITEX Dubai: CDP with AI agents
How the FTA AI agent works, and the stand scenario
1. How the FTA AI agent works
One AI agent works on top of the CDP and has two faces: the FTA agent for the FTA specialist and the FTA Assistant for the taxpayer. Both use the same CDP data; each side sees only its own. CDP rules find the issues; the agent explains, proposes and prepares. People decide and press "Send", "Submit" or "Close".
The agent works on its own between these decisions. It works with the rule results before anyone opens the screen, leads each case to the end (waits for the answer, checks it, recommends closing or escalation) and hands the case to the other side agent to agent: the FTA agent's letter reaches the taxpayer's FTA Assistant, which has the answer prepared before the taxpayer opens it.
1.1 The process

The general process: people, agents and the CDP
Five lanes: two people (FTA specialist, taxpayer), two faces of the agent (FTA agent, FTA Assistant) and the CDP. The specialist acts twice: chooses an option and approves the letter, then closes the case or escalates. The taxpayer reviews, approves and submits. Each case has its own diagram in sections 3 and 4.
1.2 Who does what: CDP and the agent
The CDP does everything that is a number or a legal rule. The agent does the reading, writing, choosing and sequencing around it, and calls the CDP for every figure. People make the decisions.

1.5 Rules for development
Numbers from data. Every figure comes from a CDP query or rule; the model writes only the text. Each answer has a "How I got this" note.
Tax rules in code. Thresholds and deadlines (VD above AED 10,000, 20 business days for a refund decision, Emirate of the establishment, tax invoice for input VAT) are business rules.
People decide. Nothing is sent, submitted or closed without a person; every step is logged.
Access by role. The FTA specialist sees the data of the role; the taxpayer sees only its own data and its counterparties.
One case, one set of facts on both sides: same number, dates, amounts.
Synthetic data only on the stand.

2. The stand
A visitor types a name and chooses a role: FTA Specialist or Business. The stand has two cases and a free query; each case is played from either side, and a visitor who plays both sees one story from two sides. A run takes 3–4 minutes per role. From the summary of one role the visitor can switch to the other.
Dates are real calendar dates and depend on the event. In this document they are set for GITEX GLOBAL 2026 (8–11 December 2026): the FTA specialist's "today" is Thursday 10.12.2026, the business "today" is Friday 11.12.2026, and answers arrive a few days later on their own. If the event moves, all dates move with it.

5. Free query
There are two places to ask, by text or voice:
About the open case, in "Ask about this case…" on every case screen. Examples: "Why Box 1b and not Box 1a?" for Al Yasmeen, "Why are the card slips not enough?" for Al Waha.
About the data in general: from the FTA specialist's briefing, or on the taxpayer's "Ask the FTA Assistant" screen. The answer is a table, list or chart with a "How I got this" note and a next step. A finding can be turned into a case.
