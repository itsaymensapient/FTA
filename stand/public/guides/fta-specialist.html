GITEX stand · process guide

FTA Specialist path

You are the FTA specialist. The Central Data Platform (CDP) has already checked overnight data. The AI agent ranks findings, explains them in plain language, and drafts actions — you decide what is sent.

Who does what

CDP / rules own the numbers (returns vs e-invoices, refund support).
AI agent briefs, explains, recommends, and drafts.
You choose the option, edit if needed, send, and close.

Demo sequence

  1. Prep — see what the agent checked before you arrived.
  2. Briefing — two high-risk events, ordered by risk (Al Yasmeen return gap, then Al Waha refund).
  3. Explain — read the reconciliation / refund split; open invoices or supplier checks.
  4. Act — pick an option (recommended path asks the taxpayer / partial approval); Send the letter.
  5. Follow-up — review the reply / VD / paper invoices; close or escalate.
  6. Summary — session outcomes; optionally try the Business role on the same cases.

Case 1 · Al Yasmeen (return vs e-invoices)

Box 1b (Dubai) shows a VAT gap of AED 486,200 from 38 October e-invoices. Net Box 14 gap is AED 412,000 after an overstatement in Box 1a. Recommended path: ask the taxpayer; on follow-up the VD explains the cut-off and emirate allocation — close when remaining difference is zero.

Case 2 · Al Waha (refund)

Refund claim AED 284,600 is only partly supported by received e-invoices. Bright Line invoices sit on hold because the supplier has not filed. Recommended path: partial approval + request documents; on follow-up approve supported paper invoices, reject unsupported amounts, hold supplier-dependent VAT.
