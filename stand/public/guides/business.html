GITEX stand · process guide

Business / FTA Assistant path

You are the VAT manager for a company. The FTA Assistant already has the FTA letter, matched it to your ledger or purchase records, and prepared a recommended reply — you check and submit.

Who does what

FTA Assistant explains the letter, finds the invoices in your records, and drafts the VD / reply pack.
You choose how to respond, edit if needed, and submit before the deadline.

Demo sequence

  1. Prep — see what the assistant prepared overnight.
  2. Choose a company — Al Yasmeen (return clarification) or Al Waha (refund documents).
  3. Check — open the overview item with its deadline.
  4. Explain — read the FTA ask vs your ledger / purchase evidence; open invoice lists.
  5. Act — pick the recommended option (VD + reply, or documents + VD); submit / send.
  6. Follow-up — read the FTA decision; finish to the summary.

Al Yasmeen · request for clarification

The FTA asks about AED 486,200 more Dubai VAT on e-invoices (AED 412,000 net). Your ledger shows 34 invoices booked late and 4 reported under the wrong emirate. Recommended path: one Voluntary Disclosure correcting both, plus a clear reply — do not double-count those invoices in the next return.

Al Waha · refund request

AED 53,600 was already supported. For the rest: six paper tax invoices (AED 150,000) are recoverable; card slips (AED 34,200) are not; Bright Line (AED 46,800) depends on the supplier filing. Recommended path: send the paper invoices + payment proof and a VD removing the non-recoverable amount.
